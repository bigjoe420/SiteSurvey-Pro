Web Serial isn’t available here — open this page in desktop Chrome or Edge over HTTPS.

Flashing takes about a minute. The board reboots into SiteSurvey Pro when done.

What you need

  • A Guition NM-CYD-C5 board and a data-capable USB–C cable
  • Desktop Chrome or Edge (Web Serial; Firefox and iOS are not supported)
  • Windows only: the CH340 USB–serial driver (usually already installed)

Steps

  1. Plug the board into your computer.
  2. Click Connect & Flash and pick the board’s serial port.
  3. Accept the erase prompt on a first install.
  4. Wait for the progress bar to finish — the device resets and boots into the survey UI.

Troubleshooting

  • No port in the list? Re-check the cable (charge-only cables are the usual culprit) and the CH340 driver.
  • Connect fails? Hold the BOOT button on the board, click Connect, release BOOT once flashing starts.
  • Prefer the command line? The same binaries flash with esptool.py — see the README.
